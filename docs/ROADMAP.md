# Roadmap

Ideas for where the second brain goes next. Roughly ordered by pull, not commitment.

## Custom podcast (idea, 2026-06-11)

Generate a personal podcast from the vault so listening becomes another review
channel — help remembering and thinking, away from the screen.

Sketch:

- Pick ideas that are due for review (or recently contested) and weave them
  into a short narrated episode: the insight, its source context, and how it
  connects to other concepts in the vault.
- Spaced-repetition aware: hearing an idea could count as a light review, or
  at least prime it for the next active-recall session.
- Possible shapes: daily 5-minute "due today" digest, per-book recap episodes,
  or a "tensions" episode where sources disagree.
- Pipeline thought: Claude writes the script from idea/concept notes, a TTS
  voice renders it, episodes land in a private RSS feed any podcast app can
  subscribe to.

## Shipped

- Review queue timelines: per-card journey (source → captured → first review →
  last review → next due), "it's been X" chips on queue cards, longest-waiting
  ideas surfaced first.
- Session progress that doesn't reset after each review ("12 of 140").
